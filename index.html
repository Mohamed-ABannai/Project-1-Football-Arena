Football arena

How to Play

  • Choose one of the available football categories.
  • Select your difficulty level: Easy, Medium, or Hard.
  • The game contains 16 cards with 8 matching pairs.
  • Click the Start button ▶ to start the game and timer.
  • Click two cards to reveal them.
  • If the two cards match, they will stay open.
  • If they do not match, they will flip back.

Difficulty Levels

  • Easy: 120 seconds.
  • Medium: 90 seconds.
  • Hard: 45 seconds.

Winning & Losing

  • You win if you match all 8 pairs before the timer reaches zero
  • You lose if the timer reaches zero before matching all the cards
  • Click the Reset button ⟳ to restart the game.
